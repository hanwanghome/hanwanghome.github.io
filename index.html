About

I recently completed my postdoctoral research at TurboNet Team of Pengcheng National Laboratory in Shenzhen, China. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Peking University advised by Prof. Hui Li.

My research focuses on the intersection of networking, data stream processing, and data intelligence. I design efficient sketch-based algorithms and probabilistic data structures for high-precision, real-time analytics over massive network and graph data streams. I am also interested in combining streaming techniques with data mining and AI to support intelligent monitoring, anomaly detection, and decision-making in high-performance systems.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral or research scientist positions in the United States. Feel free to reach out!
